How to play

First steps

Hold left click on a tree to collect logs. Press E, turn logs into planks, then make a workbench and place it. Standing next to it unlocks picks, axes, shovels, blades and gear.

Staying alive

Food drains as you move. Eat berries from leaves, roast mushrooms in a kiln, or make berry jam. At night and in dark caves, murklings hop toward you. Hit them with a blade, and light your base with lanterns. A bedroll sets your spawn point and lets you sleep through the night.

The Emberdeep

Craft an Ember gate from clay brick, coal and copper at a workbench. Place it and use it to travel to a cavern world of ashrock, magma seas, glowroots and cinder seams. Cinder makes the best tools and gear. Use the gate there to come home.

Deeper still

Emberdeep materials make a Grave gate to the misty Deathstone Wastes, where gravemoths hunt in the fog and deathstone cores hold the strongest material in the game. Deathstone and moth dust make an Aurora gate to the Last Reach, the final dimension, home of the cairnlings.

Kilns and crates

A kiln heats sand into glass, clay into brick, rubble into stone, logs into coal and mushrooms into food. Put fuel in the bottom slot. Crates hold 27 stacks.

Drag items between slots, or click to pick up and click to drop. Right-click splits a stack, double-click gathers the same item, Shift-click moves a stack across. Hover over an item and press 1–9 to swap it into your hotbar.
